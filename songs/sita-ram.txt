=== Meaning
Overall meaning:
"Glory to Sita! Glory, glory to Rama!"

The deeper picture:
What makes this chant particularly beautiful is that Sita is named first — before Rama. This is significant. Together they represent:

Their union is the marriage of consciousness and nature, of the absolute and the manifest. Chanting them together is an invocation of wholeness — the divine in its complete form, masculine and feminine, inseparable.
It is also simply a chant of pure joy — Jai Jai bubbling up like laughter, like love that can't be contained.
