=== Meaning
Full meaning:
"May all beings everywhere be happy and free."
Some expand it slightly: "May all beings in all worlds be happy, peaceful, and liberated."

Its depth:
What makes it powerful is its radical inclusivity — lokah samastah means absolutely everyone, without exception. Not just people you love, not just humans, not just your community — all beings, all realms. It's essentially a universal compassion prayer, similar in spirit to the Buddhist metta (loving-kindness) meditation.

It comes from the Yoga tradition and is rooted in the Rigveda's broader philosophy of universal well-being. It's also closely associated with the principle of ahimsa (non-harm).
Word by word:
"Lokah" — all worlds, all realms, all places; beings everywhere
"Samastah" — all, together, the whole, without exception
"Sukhino" — happy, joyful, at ease, free from suffering (sukha = happiness/ease, the opposite of dukkha)
"Bhavantu" — may they be, let it be so (a Sanskrit optative/benedictive form — a wish or blessing)
