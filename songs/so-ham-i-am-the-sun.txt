=== Meaning
This mantra comes directly from Adi Shankaracharya's Nirvana Shatakam (also called Atma Shatakam), one of the most stunning poems in Sanskrit literature, where he systematically strips away every identity until only pure awareness remains, concluding each verse with:
"Chidananda rupah Shivoham Shivoham""I am the form of pure consciousness and bliss — I am Shiva, I am Shiva"
