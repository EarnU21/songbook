title: Praise. Praise. Praise.
key: C
note: Natalie Ekenbrant

> G (3 and 4)

Praise. Praise. Praise.
Just be still.
In God's grace.

Breathe. Breathe. Breathe.
And receive.
His loving peace.

Wait. Wait. Wait.
Let Him show,
You His way.

Trust. Trust. Trust.
Do not fear.
He is here.
