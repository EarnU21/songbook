=== Meaning
Overall meaning:
"This is the sacred hour of beholding you — this season is for playing in divine nectar — carrying every breath full of joy — this is the time to live in blissful abandon."

The deeper picture:
Unlike the Sanskrit mantras which often work through sacred names and cosmic titles, this bhajan works through pure atmosphere and feeling — it describes the quality of the devotional moment rather than naming the deity directly. It is painting a scene:

It moves through the senses into something beyond them — the complete absorption in the divine moment. The word masti at the end is particularly beautiful — it suggests not a solemn religious experience but something more like ecstatic, childlike, dancing joy — the kind Radha and Krishna embody.
It feels like a song that could be sung at dawn, greeting the first light, or in a temple at the moment the deity's veil is lifted for darshan.
Breaking it down line by line:
"Tumare darshan ki bela"
Tumare = yours, of you (intimate address to the divine)
Darshan = the sacred seeing of the divine — in Hindu tradition darshan is not just looking but a mutual, grace-filled exchange of sight between devotee and deity. You don't just see God — God sees you back. It is a blessing in itself
Ki bela = the time of, the moment of, the hour of
"This is the hour of beholding you" / "The sacred moment of your darshan"
"Ye mausam ras rachene ka"
Ye = this
Mausam = season, weather, atmosphere — but used poetically as mood, time, ambience
Ras = divine juice, essence, flavor, nectar — in Vaishnava tradition rasa is the essence of devotional experience, the taste of divine love. Also refers to Krishna's Rasa Lila — the cosmic dance of love
Rachene ka = for creating, for weaving, for playing
"This season is for playing in divine nectar" / "This is the time for weaving the rasa"
"Liye ulasse ki sense"
Liye = carrying, bringing, filled with
Ulass = joy, jubilation, enthusiasm, effervescence — the bubbling up of happiness
Ki = of
Sense = likely saans or sanse = breaths, sighs — every breath
"Carrying the breaths of pure joy" / "Every breath filled with jubilation"
"Samai masti me jine ka"
Samai = time, the moment, the era
Masti = intoxication, bliss, abandon, ecstasy — but a joyful, free intoxication, not destructive. The word carries playfulness and total immersion
Me jine ka = for living in, for being alive within
"This is the time to live in divine intoxication" / "The moment to be fully alive in bliss"
