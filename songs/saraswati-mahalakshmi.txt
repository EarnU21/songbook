=== Meaning
Overall meaning:
"I bow to Saraswati, to the great Lakshmi, to Durga — I bow to the Goddess."
It is a chant of total devotion to the divine feminine in all her forms — honoring her as knowledge, as grace, and as fierce protection all at once. Chanting all three together is an invocation of complete divine feminine blessing.
Breaking it down:
"Saraswati" — goddess of wisdom, knowledge, learning, music, arts, and speech. Her name means "she who flows" — both as river (she is associated with the sacred Saraswati river) and as the flow of consciousness and creativity. She carries a veena (lute), books, and a lotus. Her color is white — pure, clear awareness.
"Mahalakshmi" — Maha = great + Lakshmi = goddess of abundance, beauty, prosperity, grace, and good fortune. She is the consort of Vishnu, the sustainer. Her name derives from laksha = to perceive, to goal. She represents both material and spiritual wealth. Her color is gold/red — warmth and abundance.
"Durga" — goddess of strength, protection, courage, and the victory of light over darkness. Her name means "the invincible" or "the fortress." She is the fierce, warrior mother who slays the buffalo demon Mahishasura. Her color is red/orange — power and fire.
"Devi" — simply "Goddess" — the divine feminine principle in its entirety. All three are ultimately expressions of the one Devi, the universal mother.
"Namaha" — "I bow, I honor, I surrender to you" — a complete offering of reverence, from the root namas
