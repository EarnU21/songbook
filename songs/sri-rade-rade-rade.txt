=== Meaning
One of the most intimate and ecstatic kirtans in the Vaishnava tradition — this chant comes from the heart of Bhakti yoga, the path of pure devotional love.
Overall meaning:
"O sacred Radha, Radha, Radha — Radha, Radha, Radha, O dark beloved Krishna — O Govinda, O Gopala, O Vasudeva, O eternal bliss!"

The deeper picture:
This kirtan lives entirely in the world of Vrindavan — the sacred eternal realm where Krishna plays with Radha and the gopis (cowherd girls). Every name here points to that landscape:

It moves from Radha — beginning with the devotee, with love — and then calls out to Krishna by name after name, each one a different facet of the same infinite beloved.
In Gaudiya Vaishnavism this is the essence of everything — Radha and Krishna, lover and beloved, devotion and its object, the wave and the ocean — endlessly calling to each other, endlessly finding each other, in the eternal play of divine love.
