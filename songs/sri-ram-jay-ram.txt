[Am]Love is the answer, [C]love is the way
Sri [G]Ram Jay Ram [Am]Jay Jay Ram

=== Meaning
„Ehre sei Ram (dem Göttlichen / der universellen Freude), Sieg für Ram.“

"Sri" – honour, beauty, the respectful address of the divine
"Ram" – Rama, the divine; also read as the joy that dwells in all things
"Jay" – victory, glory: may it prevail
