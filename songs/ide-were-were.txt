=== Meaning
This is a Yoruba/Lucumí chant dedicated to Oshun (also spelled Ochún), the Orisha of love, rivers, fertility, beauty, and sweetness in the Yoruba religion and its diaspora traditions like Santería/Lucumí (Cuba) and Candomblé (Brazil).
The chant is a prayer and praise song to Oshun, honoring her with her sacred symbols (beads, flowing water, sweetness). It essentially says: "The beads of good fortune belong to Oshun — blessings flow to her and through her."
It's one of the most well-known Oshun songs and has been popularized globally, including by Snatam Kaur and others in the world music and yoga communities.
