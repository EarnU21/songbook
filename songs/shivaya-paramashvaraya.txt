=== Meaning
Overall meaning:
"To Shiva, the Supreme Lord — to he who wears the moon — I bow, Om. To the source of all existence, from whom all qualities arise — to the one who dances the cosmic Tandava — I bow, Om."
Breaking it down:
"Shivaya"
Shiva + the dative suffix ya = "to/for Shiva"
An offering, a bow directed toward him
"Parameshwaraya"
Parama (supreme) + Ishwara (Lord) + ya (dative)
"To the Supreme Lord" — the absolute, highest reality
"Chandra Shekharaya"
Chandra = moon + Shekhara = crown, crest
"He who wears the crescent moon as his crown"
One of Shiva's most beautiful and recognizable epithets — the crescent moon adorns his matted hair (jata), symbolizing the control of time and mind, and the cooling, soothing aspect of his nature balancing his inner fire
"Namah Om"
Namah = I bow, I surrender + Om = the primordial sound
A complete act of devotional surrender, sealed with the universal vibration
"Bhavaya"
From Bhava = existence, being, the source of all that exists
"To the one who is pure existence itself"
Another name of Shiva — he is not just a being among beings, he is Being
"Guna Sambhavaya"
Guna = the three qualities of nature (sattva/rajas/tamas) + Sambhava = origin, source
"To the one from whom all qualities arise"
Shiva as the ground of all manifestation — the source from which the entire fabric of existence emerges
"Shiva Tandavaya"
Tandava = the cosmic dance of Shiva (Nataraja)
"To the one who performs the Tandava"
The Tandava is Shiva's legendary cosmic dance — simultaneously the dance of creation, destruction, and liberation. It is wild, ecstatic, and total. The universe itself pulses to its rhythm
