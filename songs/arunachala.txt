=== Meaning
This is a deeply sacred chant connected to one of the most spiritually charged places on earth — Arunachala, the sacred mountain in Tamil Nadu, South India. This chant sits at the intersection of Shaivism, Advaita Vedanta, and living mystical tradition.
The deeper picture:
What makes this chant unique among all the ones in this conversation is its connection to a specific physical place that is simultaneously a state of consciousness:

Ramana Maharshi taught that Arunachala is the Self — not a symbol of it, not a pointer to it, but the Self itself in mountain form. To chant its name is to turn attention back toward the source of awareness — the same movement as asking "Who am I?"

Overall meaning:
"Om — O radiant, unmoving one — Arunachala, Arunachala, Arunachala — Shiva, Shiva — it is Shiva — Shiva."
But underneath the words the meaning is simply:
Pure awareness, recognizing itself.
