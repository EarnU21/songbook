=== Meaning
A joyful shift in energy! After the profound depths of the Pavamana Mantra, this kirtan brings pure celebration and delight — it has the feeling of children dancing in the street.
Overall meaning:
"Bambole! Bambole! Glory to Sita Rama! Glory to Hanuman!"

The deeper picture:
What is beautiful about this kirtan coming after all the profound Sanskrit philosophy is that it reminds us that bhakti is not only serious. The path of devotion includes:

All three are equally valid, equally deep. Hanuman himself embodies this — he is simultaneously the greatest philosopher (his grammar was said to be perfect) and the greatest fool of love — tearing open his chest to show Rama and Sita living inside his heart.
Bambole is that torn-open heart — beyond words, beyond philosophy, just pure joy spilling out.
