=== Meaning
This is a Hindu devotional chant (bhajan/kirtan) praising Durga, the great mother goddess.
"Glory to the Mother of the Universe! Glory, glory, Mother! Glory to Mother Durga!"
It's a very simple, open-hearted chant meant to be repeated in a meditative, devotional way — the repetition itself is the practice.
"Jay / Jaya" — means "victory to" or "glory to," a traditional Sanskrit salutation of praise and triumph. You'll find it at the start of countless Hindu devotional phrases.
"Jagatambe" — from Sanskrit: Jagat (the world/universe) + Amba (mother). So it means "Mother of the Universe." This is one of Durga's most beloved epithets.
"Ma" — simply "Mother," an intimate, heartfelt address to the goddess.
"Durga" — the fierce, protective mother goddess, associated with strength, protection, and the victory of good over evil. Her name itself means "the one who is difficult to approach" or "the fortress."
